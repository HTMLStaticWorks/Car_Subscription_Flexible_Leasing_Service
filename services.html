Flexible Car Subscription & Leasing

Seasonal Vehicle Swapping & Doorstep Exchange.

Never be locked into one vehicle for years again. With ORVEXA's all-inclusive car subscription, switch seamlessly between electric sedans, luxury SUVs, and sports coupes whenever your lifestyle or seasonal needs change. Book in your subscriber app, and our concierge delivers your freshly detailed vehicle right to your driveway while collecting your current one.

  • Multi-Category Freedom: Drive an electric fastback for city commutes, a luxury SUV for winter trips, or a performance coupe for weekends.
  • White-Glove Flatbed Handover: Enclosed transport delivers your sanitized, fully charged or fueled vehicle directly to your residence or workplace.
  • Zero Transfer Paperwork: Comprehensive insurance, registration, and routine maintenance roll over automatically with zero hidden exchange fees.
  • Up to 4 Swaps Per Year: Available on Select & Premium tiers, backed by flexible 30-day minimum commitments.
