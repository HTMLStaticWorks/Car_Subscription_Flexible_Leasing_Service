The Business Model

What is a Car Subscription?

A car subscription is the modern bridge between inflexible multi-year leasing and expensive short-term car rentals. Instead of buying a car and worrying about financing interest, rapid depreciation, insurance premiums, and unpredictable repairs, you pay one predictable monthly fee.

Whether you need an electric vehicle for 3 months, a luxury sedan for 6 months, or wish to swap into a mountain-ready SUV for the winter ski season, ORVEXA puts your entire garage in your pocket.
