Everything you need to know about our subscription tiers, lease periods, and all-inclusive benefits.

Our fleet spans compact city cars (Mini Cooper S), executive sedans (BMW 5 Series, Audi A3), luxury family SUVs (Range Rover Velar, BMW X5, Audi Q5), high-performance EVs (Porsche Taycan 4S, Tesla Model 3 Long Range), and sports icons (Porsche 911, Toyota GR Supra). Essential tier covers compact/midsize sedans; Flex tier covers crossovers and SUVs; Premium tier unlocks luxury sedans and EVs; Performance tier unlocks sports exotics.

Our minimum lease period is 30 days (1 month). The maximum continuous lease period is 24 months with automatic rollover. You can choose from 1, 3, 6, or 12–24 month commitments. With month-to-month plans, you can cancel or return the car with just 7 calendar days' written notice and zero exit penalties.

Every ORVEXA subscription is 100% all-inclusive: comprehensive insurance with collision coverage, all scheduled factory maintenance (oil, brakes, wiper blades, fluids), tire replacement for normal wear and tear, 24/7 roadside assistance, digital concierge management, doorstep delivery within 48 hours, and complimentary vehicle swaps on qualified plans.
